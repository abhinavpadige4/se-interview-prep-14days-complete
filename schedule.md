# 14-Day Software Engineering Interview Preparation Plan

## Overview
This plan covers Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA), System Design, and Behavioral interview preparation over 14 days, starting from 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-28.

## Daily Schedule Structure
- **DSA**: 2 hours daily
- **System Design**: 1.5 hours daily  
- **Behavioral**: 0.5 hours daily
- **Breaks & Review**: 1 hour daily
- **Total**: 5 hours/day
