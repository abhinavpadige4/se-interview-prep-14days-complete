## Behavioral Interview Preparation

### Frameworks & Methods
- **STAR Method** 
  - Situation: Describe the context
  - Task: Explain your responsibility
  - Action: Detail what you did
  - Result: Share the outcome with metrics

- **PAR Method** (Problem, Action, Result)
  - Simplified version of STAR
  - Good for quicker responses

### Common Behavioral Questions Categories
1. **Teamwork & Collaboration**
   - Tell me about a time you worked with a difficult team member
   - Describe a situation where you had to compromise
   - Give an example of when you helped a struggling teammate

2. **Leadership & Initiative**
   - Tell me about a time you took initiative without being asked
   - Describe when you had to lead a project or team
   - Tell me about a time you improved a process

3. **Problem Solving & Decision Making**
   - Describe a difficult problem you solved
   - Tell me about a time you made a wrong decision
   - Give an example of when you had to think outside the box

4. **Adaptability & Learning**
   - Tell me about a time you had to learn something quickly
   - Describe when you failed and what you learned
   - Tell me about adapting to a major change

5. **Motivation & Culture Fit**
   - Why do you want to work here?
   - What motivates you in your work?
   - Describe your ideal work environment

### Story Preparation Guide
1. **Identify 5-7 key stories** from your experience
2. **Cover different competencies** (leadership, teamwork, problem-solving, etc.)
3. **Quantify results** whenever possible (%, $, time saved, etc.)
4. **Practice out loud** to improve delivery
5. **Prepare for follow-up questions** on each story

## Daily Resource Usage Guide

### Days 1-3 (Foundations)
- DSA: NeetCode Arrays & Hashing videos
- System Design: System Design Primer intro sections
- Behavioral: STAR method explanation and practice

### Days 4-6 (Intermediate)
- DSA: Striver's SDE Sheet linked list/tree problems
- System Design: Watch Alex Xu videos on caching and databases
- Behavioral: Practice 2-3 STAR stories

### Days 7-9 (Advanced)
- DSA: NeetCode binary search/heap/graph videos
- System Design: Study system design case studies
- Behavioral: Mock behavioral interviews

### Days 10-12 (Application)
- DSA: Practice DP and trie problems
- System Design: Work through case studies
- Behavioral: Refine stories with feedback

### Days 13-14 (Review & Mock)
- DSA: Mixed review of weak areas
- System Design: Full system design mock
- Behavioral: Full behavioral mock interview
